# Changelog

## 0.1.0 - 2026-11-11
- First beta: tap the whole tile to toggle a selected Home Assistant input boolean.
- Show a live timer countdown in red while heating is enabled, plus boiler temperature and on/off state.
- Handle paused, idle and unavailable timers without starting a local timer or changing boiler settings.
- English, German, Portuguese and Dutch interface; English store preview.
